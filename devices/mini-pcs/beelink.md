## Families

| Family | Position | Verify |
|---|---|---|
| Mini S | Entry-level | CPU generation, SATA/NVMe support |
| EQ / EQi | Efficient office/server | RAM type, integrated PSU, NIC speed |
| SE / SER | Mainstream performance | Exact AMD/Intel model, USB4, cooling |
| GT / GTi / GTR | High performance | Dock/eGPU support, PSU, thermals |
| ME mini | Storage/NAS hybrid | Drive count, network ports, storage cooling |

Family names are reused. For example, two SER or EQ machines can have different processors, ports, memory layouts, or wireless adapters.

## Record an exact unit

- Full underside-label model and serial-prefix format
- CPU, RAM layout, SSD model, and free slots
- Ethernet, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, audio, and USB controllers
- BIOS version and matching vendor download
- Power adapter voltage/wattage
- Tested OS, kernel, suspend, display, audio, network, and virtualization
- Recovery image, driver backup, and restore method
